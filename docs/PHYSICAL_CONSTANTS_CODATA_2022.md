The complete captured matrix is checked for positive semidefiniteness with a bounded Jacobi eigenvalue oracle; no unknown entries are filled with zeros. Published coefficients are rounded to five decimals; this selected matrix remains PSD within 1e-12. Perfect-correlation subspaces are singular by construction, so strict positive definiteness would be the wrong requirement. Use the published nominal/u precision; dependencies between separately rounded recommended constants can leave small residuals.

## Scientific scope and authorities

This implements a bounded first-order sensitivity/covariance subset, not full GUM conformance. User inputs specify standard uncertainty (k=1); separate new inputs are independent by product contract. No probability distribution, effective degrees of freedom, confidence level, or guaranteed interval is inferred.

- [NIST values and version history](https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/Reference/versioncon.shtml): web 9.0 uses the 2022 adjustment, released 9 May 2024. [Publication](https://www.nist.gov/publications/codata-recommended-values-fundamental-physical-constants-2022): 30 April 2025; data through 31 December 2022.
- [JCGM 100:2008](https://doi.org/10.59161/JCGM100-2008E), §§5.1–5.2: local sensitivities and covariance; §7.2.6: uncertainty usually needs at most two significant digits, estimate rounded consistently, extra calculation digits retained. NumOS chooses two digits for compact reporting, with a separate inspection view and unrounded canonical payload.
- [JCGM 100:2008/Amd.1:2026](https://doi.org/10.59161/PPDI3267), amendment to §4.1.4 and H.1.7: significant nonlinearity can affect both central estimate and uncertainty; higher-order or Monte Carlo treatment may be needed. NumOS rejects unsupported significant nonlinearity instead of inventing zero uncertainty.
- [JCGM GUM-6:2020](https://doi.org/10.59161/JCGMGUM-6-2020), §12 and Annex F.2: model adequacy and a limited local comparison against the linearized model at xi±u(xi). This local check is not a proof for arbitrary nonlinear functions. A 10% remainder threshold and ±3u domain guards, if used, are NumOS product choices, not prescribed GUM thresholds.
- [JCGM 101:2008](https://doi.org/10.59161/JCGM101-2008): distribution propagation by Monte Carlo is an alternative when linearization is inadequate; it is not implemented in production.
- [NIST TN 1297 §7](https://www.nist.gov/pml/nist-technical-note-1297/nist-tn-1297-7-reporting-uncertainty): identify standard uncertainty or expanded uncertainty and k; probability interpretation needs support. U=2u is labelled k=2 and does not automatically mean 95% confidence.
