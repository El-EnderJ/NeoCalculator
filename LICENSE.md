# Licensing

This repository is a multi-license distribution; there is no single license
that replaces every component's terms.

- NumOS/NeoCalculator-authored software carrying the project notice is
  offered under **GPL-3.0-or-later**. The complete GPLv3 text is in
  `LICENSE-SOFTWARE`. The explicit project-wide GPL notice first appeared on
  2026-04-27; earlier repository history advertised MIT. That transition and
  the AI-authored contribution history are disclosed in
  `docs/LICENSING_AUDIT_2026.md` rather than described as a mere
  clarification.
- NeoCalculator hardware design sources are licensed under
  **CERN-OHL-S-2.0**. The complete text is in `LICENSE-HARDWARE`.
- Vendored Giac/KhiCAS is modified GPL-3.0-or-later software. Its provenance
  and local changes are documented in `lib/giac/NUMOS_CHANGES.md`.
- Font software and font-derived generated data remain under **OFL-1.1**.
  Their copyright/license files and exact provenance are under
  `assets/fonts/`.
- Lua, LibTomMath, TinyMT, CLAPACK/f2c, GL2PS material, and package-manager
  dependencies retain their respective licenses.

See `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` for component boundaries, notices, upstream
identifiers, and links to the license texts stored in this repository.

SPDX expressions on mixed generated headers describe the material in that
file; they do not dual-license the font software or relicense third-party code.
