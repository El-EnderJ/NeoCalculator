## Brightness-zero contract and physical acceptance

Final user-requested semantics supersede the earlier temporary-off proposal:

- Settings range is **1..192**, so Settings never deliberately blacks out the
  ordinary UI.
- Values 1..192 are persistable; 192 remains the existing validated maximum.
- A requested/legacy version-3 value of zero migrates to SAFE low brightness
  32 and is rewritten once.
- Version-2 records have no brightness field and preserve the SAFE default.
- Corrupt/out-of-range values cannot become a valid black boot.
- LEFT/RIGHT and autorepeat alter PWM only; persistence occurs once when
  leaving Settings if the final value changed.
- RESET or power loss before leaving retains the previously committed visible
  value.
